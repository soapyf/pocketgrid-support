## About PocketGrid

PocketGrid keeps you connected to Second Life on mobile without loading 3D worlds, meshes, or heavy textures—minimizing battery drain and mobile data usage.

### Current Features (v0.9.0 Beta)
- **Messaging:** Local chat (including custom channels), 1-on-1 direct messages, and optional group or ad-hoc conference chats.
- **Nearby & Social:** Nearby resident radar with chat/shout distance indicators, resident profiles and notes, contacts/friend permissions, groups, and block list management.
- **Travel:** World map region search, live resident counts, favorite places, landmarks, teleports, Teleport Home, and teleport offer handling.
- **Appearance:** Switch outfits or view and detach currently worn items (`COF`).
- **Mobile-First Security & Notifications:** Android Keystore-encrypted credential storage, per-account encrypted local message history, 30-day MFA device trust, and customizable background notifications.

## Reporting a Bug or Requesting a Feature

Please check existing [Issues](https://github.com/soapyf/pocketgrid-support/issues) first, then open a new issue including:
1. Your **Android version** and **device model** (and PocketGrid version, e.g. `0.9.0`).
2. Clear steps to reproduce the issue (or what you expected to happen).
3. Whether you were on **Wi-Fi or cellular data**, and whether the app was in the foreground or background.

> **Privacy Reminder:** Never post your Second Life password, MFA codes, or private DMs in public GitHub issues. For account-specific or sensitive reports, email **pocketgrid@proton.me**.

## FAQ

**Why don't I see 3D graphics or hear voice?**  
PocketGrid is intentionally a **text-only** client designed for messaging, social interaction, travel, and outfit switching with minimal battery and cellular data use.

**Does PocketGrid store my password or messages on any developer server?**  
No. PocketGrid connects directly from your phone to Second Life (or your selected grid). See [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for full details.

**What if background messages stop arriving after my screen has been off for a long time?**  
Android's aggressive battery optimization (Doze mode) can pause background network access on some devices. If this happens reliably on your phone, disabling battery optimization ("Unrestricted") for PocketGrid in Android App Info helps keep background notifications responsive.
